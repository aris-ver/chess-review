Privacy policy

Version of 22 October 2026

In short. Blundr runs on your own computer, in your browser. Without an account, nothing about you is stored on our server: your games, reviews and puzzle progress stay in your browser. If you create an account, we store only what is needed to sign you in, your puzzle progress and the list of profiles you added, so they follow you to other browsers. Your reviewed games and their analysis are never uploaded. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Aris Verikokkos, Chora Naxou, 84300 Naxos, Greece. Contact for anything about your data: blundr.gr@gmail.com. Blundr is a free, non-commercial project.

2. Using Blundr without an account

  • In your browser only. The profiles you add, your games and their reviews, the engine's analysis, your settings and your puzzle progress are kept in your browser's storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) on your device. We cannot see them. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them.
  • Fetching games. When you add a chess.com or Lichess username, your browser asks chess.com or Lichess for that player's public games directly. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.
  • Delivering the site. The site is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the service from attacks, Cloudflare necessarily processes your IP address and technical request data. We do not keep access logs. Part of the software (Pyodide, the Python runtime) is loaded from the jsDelivr network, and the full chess engine (Stockfish) from the unpkg network; they too see your IP address when they serve those files.

4. Legal basis

We process account data because it is necessary to provide the account you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Delivering the site and keeping it secure is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The cookies we use are strictly necessary for the service you request, so they need no consent (section 8).

5. Where your data is kept, and who else handles it

  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc. and its EU entities) hosts the site and the account database as our processor, under its data processing agreement. The account database is kept and run only inside the European Union. Cloudflare may process technical data in other countries, including the USA, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends the sign-in codes as our processor, under its data processing agreement. To do that it handles your email address and the message.
  • Lichess or Google handle your sign-in on their own sites, under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data, puzzle progress and the profile list: until you delete your account (a profile you remove is removed from the account at the next sync). An account not used for 24 months is deleted automatically, with all its data.
  • Sessions: they end when you log out, or 90 days after you last used them.
  • A sign-in in progress (the short-lived record and cookie while you are at Lichess or Google), an emailed code, and the count of failed log-ins: at most 15 minutes.
  • Deleted data may remain in Cloudflare's database backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. In the account dialog (the person icon at the top right) you can:

  • Download my data: everything we store about you, as a JSON file;
  • Delete account: your account and all its data are deleted from our database at once;
  • log out, and optionally clear the puzzle progress from the browser you are using.

For anything else, write to blundr.gr@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Greece the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr, Kifisias 1-3, 115 23 Athens), or the one where you live or work.

9. Age

You must be at least 15 years old to create an account (the age of digital consent in Greece, Law 4624/2019, Article 21). Anyone can use Blundr without an account.

10. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Session cookies cannot be read by scripts and are stored on our side only in scrambled form. Access to the database is limited to the operator.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with its date. If a change affects what we store about account holders, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
